## 4. Description
T&F Fantasy is a free fantasy sports app built around real high school track and field. Start a league with friends from your school, draft real athletes, and earn points from what they actually do at meets.

CREATE OR JOIN A LEAGUE
- Start a league for your school, or join one with an invite code.
- Several leagues can run for the same school, each with its own teams, rosters and standings.
- Leagues need an even number of teams before the draft can begin.

DRAFT REAL ATHLETES
- Live draft with pick timers, a draft queue, auto-pick and several draft order options.
- Practice anytime with mock drafts.
- When the draft ends, your lineup is set automatically.

MEET-BASED MATCHUPS
- Your commissioner schedules the meets. Each meet is one head-to-head matchup.
- Rosters lock when the meet starts.
- Follow points live during the meet. Results are reviewed before the matchup becomes final.

MANAGE YOUR TEAM
- Set lineups, use the lineup optimizer and move athletes to and from IR.
- Claim free agents through weekly waivers and propose trades with other teams.
- See athlete status (Active, Questionable, Doubtful, Out, IR) on rosters and player pages.

SCORING YOU CAN TRUST
- Points come only from real, recorded performances.
- Projected points are estimates to help you plan. They never count toward your score.
- Every league uses the same scoring rules, and finished matchups keep their scores.

SEASONS AND HISTORY
- Indoor and outdoor seasons are tracked separately.
- Finished seasons are archived with standings, matchups, rosters, transactions and the champion, so you can look back anytime.

FOR COMMISSIONERS
- Commissioner Tools to manage teams, meets, athletes and league settings.
- Import a school roster from a pasted list or spreadsheet and review possible duplicates before anything is saved.
- A searchable Commissioner Guide explains every tool.

STAY IN THE LOOP
- Notifications for draft picks, trades, waivers and meet results.
- Live Activity on the Lock Screen during a live meet.

T&F Fantasy is free, with no purchases, wagering or prizes. It's just fantasy points and bragging rights.

## 12. App Review notes
Purpose and audience: T&F Fantasy is a free fantasy sports app for high school track and field. Friends from a school form a private league, draft real athletes, and earn points from real meet results. No money, prizes or wagering are involved.
How to review:
1. Sign in with the demo account in the Sign-In Information fields.
2. Open the demo league from My Leagues.
3. Matchup tab: the current meet matchup with ACTUAL, PENDING and FINALIZED labels. Projected points are labeled PROJ and never count.
4. Team tab: roster, lineup and athlete status. Players tab: free agents and waiver claims.
5. Settings: league info, Past Seasons, "How T&F Fantasy works", and Delete Account.
6. Commissioner Tools (demo account is commissioner): meets, teams, athletes and the Commissioner Guide.
Sign-in: An account is required to join a league. Create one in the app with email and password. Delete it in Settings > Delete Account; commissioners pick another member to take over first.
External services: Our own server stores accounts and league data. OneSignal sends push notifications (device push token and an internal user ID). Results come from public meet results pages and spreadsheets entered by commissioners.
In-app purchases: None.
Regional differences: Works the same in all regions.
Permissions: Notifications (draft, trade, waiver and meet alerts). Face ID (optional, to show the saved password in Account Settings). Photo library access through the system photo picker, to choose a team logo or athlete photo.

## 14. Privacy label (based on what the app actually collects)
Tracking: No. No data is used for tracking or shared with data brokers. No ads, no analytics SDKs.

Data linked to the user (used for App Functionality only):
- Contact Info: Email address, Name (display name).
- User Content: Photos (team logos and athlete photos you upload), Other user content (team names, league names, lineups, trades).
- Identifiers: User ID (account ID), Device ID (OneSignal push token).

Not collected: location, contacts, health, financial info, browsing history, search history, purchases, diagnostics.
